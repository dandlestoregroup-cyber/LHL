# Mature Demo review

Question: **Would I understand what Little Hut looks like after years of real operation?**

Verdict: **Yes — for the operating shape and commercial flow.** The Demo now reads as a functioning hospitality network rather than a setup checklist.

## What is immediately legible

- Public collection: three sealed, requestable homes with only independently proven Moments.
- Supply engine: every stage from sourced lead through Live, plus paused and declined outcomes.
- Partner network: owners, scouts, operators, independent assessors, and a community authority with different powers.
- Independent standard: scheduled, passed, and condition-blocked assessments with evidence and gate counts.
- Owner layer: home portfolio, reserved go/defer/decline decision, rate floor, payout state, and demand visibility.
- Booking operation: enquiries distributed across qualification, availability, quote, hold, payment, community approval, confirmation, completion, and closed outcomes.
- Commercial reality: active holds, recorded payments, a pending community case, and an approved case with evidence.
- Operational control: the active Demo or Live mode is visible in the top ribbon, toggle, record marks, footer, and fixed watermark.

## Integrity read

- Every Demo record is marked synthetic in both data and UI.
- Live starts empty and cannot borrow Demo homes, partners, counts, reviews, payments, or enquiries.
- A direct URL cannot expose a non-public property.
- No public card displays price; the operator sees and enforces the owner floor.
- Payment and confirmation remain subordinate to an active hold and required community approval.

## Deliberately honest boundary

This branch demonstrates the mature operating model and its workflows. Live is a truth-only empty workspace; it does not pretend that production identities, database records, payments, or community integrations already exist.
